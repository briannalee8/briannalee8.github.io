### CDC's MAT-LINK Clinical Surveillance System

Maternal and Infant Clinical Network (MAT-LINK) is a national public health surveillance system, monitoring major pregnancy exposures, using electronic health records. MAT-LINK is currently collecting data for the second collection cycle and more information can be found [here](https://www.cdc.gov/mat-link/about/index.html).

Brianna made major contribution's to MAT-LINK's collection archetecture, scientific priorities and data quality improvements.
